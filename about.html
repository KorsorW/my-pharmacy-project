About Kingdom of God Pharmacy

Located in Foya District, we are dedicated to providing quality drugs, expert advice and compassionate service. Our pharmacy is registered and staffed by trained professionals who follow safety standards in dispensing medicines.

Our Mission

To make safe and effective medicines accessible and affordable for the local community while promoting responsible use of medications.

Our Services

  • Prescription dispensing
  • Over-the-counter medicines
  • Advice and counseling by trained staff
  • Home delivery within service area
