Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Use the Order form to send an email with your requested medicines. We will confirm availability and delivery details.

Can I attach a prescription?

Yes. After sending the order, reply to the confirmation email with an attachment or bring a copy when receiving delivery.

What are the delivery hours?

Delivery hours are between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

What if a medicine is out of stock?

We will notify you and offer a substitute or refund where appropriate.
